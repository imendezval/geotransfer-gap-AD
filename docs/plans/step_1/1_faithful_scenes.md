# Step 1: Faithful scenes under the BehaviorBench evaluator

## Objective

Make a converted inD scene faithful to its source recording under the **actual BehaviorBench evaluation configuration**. A binary that loads or replays under another configuration is insufficient. This step establishes the admission gate that Step 2 will apply to every candidate.

## Work sequence

1. **Freeze and record the evaluation contract.** Store the BehaviorBench commit, patched-runtime hash, `evaluation.ini` hash, source recording/map hashes, scene JSON and binary hashes, ego ID, goal rule, traffic mode, and episode length in one machine-readable report. Require geometry and semantic audit reports for research-mode runs; missing or stale reports must fail the gate.
2. **Trace every premature disappearance.** Record each actor's source validity and simulator state at every step, including its removal cause: source exit, goal, collision, off-road event, or episode reset. Diagnose the known failures first: r02 track 107 and the early losses or resets in full04, full13, and full21. Report the first failing step and cause per actor.
3. **Separate ego and background lifecycles.** Permit the ego to end the episode on a legitimate goal. Keep expert background actors at their recorded positions while source-valid and while the episode is running. Give interactive background actors plausible continuation goals; a recorded end point within the simulator's goal radius must not remove an actor early. Verify late births, exits, and repeated resets after changes.
4. **Audit physical boundaries and stationary actors.** Determine which Lanelet2 curbs are actual collision boundaries before encoding them as `ROAD_EDGE`. Compare source boxes to evaluator collision boxes. Check that stationary and temporarily stopped vehicles remain visible and collidable without creating artificial off-road events. Retain all relevant source actors; any crop is an explicit, audited exception.
5. **Probe counterfactual traffic.** On a few source-faithful scenes, perturb the ego and inspect expert, IDM, and another supported traffic mode. Source expert replay establishes reconstruction fidelity; perturbations test whether the resulting closed-loop traffic remains usable. Record ghost actors, unexpected disappearance, and early reset separately from ordinary traffic-model differences.
6. **Turn the checks into one admission report.** Run the same mandatory checks on the accepted technical scene and on the known failure fixtures. Store pass/fail and exclusion reasons beside each binary. A diagnostic run with changed termination settings cannot satisfy the research gate.
7. **Resolve physical discrepancies against the source.** Align the inD background image, actor boxes, and Lanelet2 geometry in the same coordinates. Review the low-curb contacts and source-box overlaps that exclude r02, full04, and full21. For each discrepancy, establish whether it comes from the source annotation, conversion, collision geometry, or an actual physical boundary. Change a general encoding or collision rule only when the source evidence supports it; otherwise record a specific exclusion.
8. **Continue interactive-traffic debugging.** Reproduce the first IDM and other-mode loss in each failing fixture, including the actor's source box, evaluator box, boundary or collision partner, heading, and velocity immediately before loss. Test general converter or runtime corrections and rerun all regression fixtures after each change. Define what the third traffic mode is expected to preserve; do not treat zero-action dynamics as constant-velocity replay. If a model cannot preserve a scene's lifecycle, document the model limitation and exclude that scene from evaluations using the model.
9. **Verify goal and terminal-event timing.** Trace the final evaluator steps of the PDM/IDM smoke run that ended 0.01 m from the goal but reported `goal_reached=False`. Determine whether the discrepancy follows from episode-horizon timing, goal-radius evaluation, reset, or metric aggregation. Verify expert and planner goal events under the same evaluator settings. Keep the ego goal rule independent of the planner's outcome.
10. **Find, hand-check, and freeze an intersection scene.** Screen further inD windows with prespecified maneuver and source-quality criteria, then apply the same conversion and admission checks without actor-specific changes. Inspect the accepted candidate against the source recording for coordinates, sampling, all relevant actors, map topology, route, goal, physical contacts, and interactive traffic. Rerun the gate in `bb_pufferdrive` on that scene and the known failures; preserve current contracts, reports, and exclusion reasons beside the binaries.

## Completion requirements

- The gate runs against the same configuration used for planner evaluation, and verifies current source, map, binary, runtime, and configuration hashes.
- Every retained actor has the correct birth state and no premature presence. Background actors remain present through their source-valid interval while the episode runs, and leave only for a documented source exit or justified simulator event. The ego ends only at a justified goal or other reported terminal event.
- No unexplained preterminal reset, expert-ego collision, or off-road event remains in an admitted scene. Physical boundary and collision-box choices are checked against the source rather than adjusted merely to make a run pass.
- Position and heading replay errors stay below `0.01 m` and `0.01 rad`; birth velocity error stays below `0.01 m/s`. Any tolerance for an actor's last samples has an event-based justification rather than a blanket allowance.
- The known failing fixtures either pass after a justified correction or are excluded with a specific source-based reason. The accepted fixtures also pass repeated reset and traffic perturbation checks without ghost actors or premature disappearance.
- At least one **intersection** scene, not only a straight-road scene, passes technical admission under the real evaluator. Its physical-boundary and source-box findings have source-based resolutions, and its interactive-traffic modes satisfy explicitly defined lifecycle checks.
- Goal, terminal, and final-distance reporting are consistent or have a demonstrated evaluator-level explanation. A planner-specific goal adjustment cannot be used to make the scene pass.

## Persistence requirement

Continue resolving failures revealed by these steps, including new failures found during regression, until Step 1 meets every completion requirement. A failed candidate, difficult debugging, a finite set of unsuccessful experiments, or an incomplete report is not a stopping condition: diagnose the result, test a general correction or a new source-qualified candidate, and rerun the affected checks. Preserve a failing gate when evidence does not justify a correction.

Stop for user input only if a genuine blocker requires the user's intervention **and** no independent Step 1 work remains possible. Record the blocker, the evidence that it cannot be resolved locally, the exact input needed, and the other Step 1 avenues already pursued. Continue any independent work while that input is pending.

**Step 1 is complete only when an admitted scene can be reproduced and defended under the evaluator's real settings.** Its map-rule suitability for a geographic planner comparison is additionally required by Step 2.
