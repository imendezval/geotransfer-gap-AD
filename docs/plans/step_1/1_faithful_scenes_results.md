## Work against the six criteria

1. **Evaluation contract.** `scene_contract.py` records SHA-256 of the CSVs, both maps, JSON, binary, manifest, evaluator INI, converter, runtime patch, and validators. It also records the BehaviorBench commit, runtime build hash, ego, goal, traffic mode, and evaluator settings. `run_pilot_eval.py` now refuses missing geometry and semantics audits, stale source maps, and stale or failed admission reports. Technical smoke testing can still be requested explicitly with `GEOTRANSFER_ALLOW_UNVALIDATED=1`; it cannot pass research admission.

2. **Premature disappearance trace.** `trace_eval_lifecycle.py` stores per-step source validity, simulator presence, position and heading error, collision state, and first loss cause. Before the runtime correction, r02 track 107 was off-road at reset, full04 track 13 went off-road, full13 background vehicles reached their goals early, and full21 reset early. The same trace on regenerated variants now records only justified ego-goal removal in expert mode, with no background loss or early reset. A trace remains available for IDM to distinguish model failures from conversion failures.

3. **Separate lifecycles.** The isolated BehaviorBench runtime patch exempts expert background actors from goal, collision, and off-road removal while their recorded trajectory is valid. The ego keeps normal evaluator metrics and termination. Moving non-ego vehicles receive a generic goal projected beyond their source exit; every source-valid sample remains more than 4 m from that goal. The converter retains all source actors in these fixtures, and `validate_spawn.py` checks birth position, heading, velocity, prebirth state, and postexit absence under the evaluator INI. It now also accepts scenes with zero late births. `validate_parked.py` follows recorded parked positions until the ego's legitimate terminal event. These rules contain no recording-specific actor IDs.

4. **Physical map and parked actors.** `audit_physical_source.py` reports Lanelet2 boundary tags touched by source-scale actor boxes and any source-box overlaps. It uses the evaluator collision scale of 1.0 and retains high curbs, low curbs, and road borders. Parked cars remain collidable background objects and follow their source samples. The r00 ego has no boundary contact or source-box overlap. The r02 truck and full04 actor 13 contact low curbs in the source; r02 and full21 also have source-box overlaps. The exact legal/physical interpretation of low-curb contact has **not** been established from source imagery. No curb was erased to obtain a passing result.

5. **Counterfactual traffic.** `probe_counterfactual.py` shifts the ego laterally by 1 m and checks expert, IDM, and zero-action dynamics for ghosts, missing source-valid traffic, postexit presence, and early reset. r00 passes all three. The synthetic lead-vehicle diagnostic shows IDM can react: a vehicle placed 12 m behind an injected ego slowed by about 3 m/s over five steps. That diagnostic does not certify a source-faithful interaction. r02 fails after an immediate IDM truck/pedestrian collision that is absent from the source at that sample; full04 loses actor 13 off-road at step 45; full13 loses traffic in zero-action dynamics; full21 passes the perturbation but has source-box overlaps. Several additional turning candidates also failed this check, usually when source vehicles were already touching low curbs. The later source-only screening found the admitted intersection described below.

6. **Admission.** `admit_scene.py` refreshes the validators, checks exact hashes and evaluator settings, requires zero expert background losses and no prebirth/postexit ghosts, checks replay errors below 0.01 m and 0.01 rad, checks a justified ego-goal event, and records separate `technical_admitted` and `research_admitted` results. The technical gate is conservative about source-box overlaps. It now requires three event-aware repeated resets for scenes with late births. The semantics audit is mandatory and must pass for research admission. The r00 straight-road scene and the later intersection turn pass technical admission. The known failure fixtures and screened turning scenes retain explicit exclusion reasons beside their binaries.

The canonical `bb_pufferdrive` environment reran the gates. `run_pilot_eval.py --help` passed its technical guard; temporarily withholding `map_audit.json` made it fail, and the file was restored. The original r00 PDM/IDM smoke run reported `goal_reached=False` and displayed 0.01 "m" from goal. A new per-step trace proves the true final distance was **2.0607 m**, outside the 2 m goal radius. BehaviorBench's observation scales goal coordinates by 0.005, and its evaluator displays the normalized value without converting back to metres. The goal event itself was correct; `audit_goal_reporting.py` records this reconciliation. Do not treat the displayed final distance as metres until its evaluator reporting is corrected.

## Continuation: admitted intersection turn

The source-only `find_turn_candidates.py` screened inD vehicle tracks by turn angle, path length, and displacement. A balanced batch of 32 windows across four locations was converted with the same no-crop, parked-actor, late-spawn, and goal rules. Three passed the source geometry and physical prefilter. Of those, recording 04 / ego 133 passed expert, IDM, and zero-action dynamics perturbation without ghosts, source-valid traffic loss, postexit presence, or early reset. The other two lost traffic under zero-action dynamics and remain excluded.

The accepted turn is visible in `pilot/validated/faithful_intersection04/testing/source_image_registration.png`: the ego follows a right turn through the T intersection on the aerial image, while mapped curbs align with the visible road edges. The image registration is an estimated translation using source resolution and map-edge alignment; it is evidence for manual review, not a survey-grade coordinate transform. The ego and moving traffic have no audited curb contact, and there is no source-box overlap. All eight retained actors include three parked vehicles, one pedestrian, two late vehicles, and another moving vehicle. The ego route goes from lanelet 1128 to 1105, and the goal is near the mapped lane centerline.

The promoted scene's current `admission.json` has 37 checks, with only `semantic_screen` false. Expert traffic has no background loss or preterminal reset; the ego is removed at its justified goal at step 83. Maximum source replay errors are `0.00001085 m` and `0.000000237 rad`. All three repeated reset cycles reproduce the same actors and ego goal step with no premature or missing background actor. A guarded one-map PDM/IDM evaluator run reaches the goal with no collision or off-road event. These are technical fidelity checks, not a geographic planner comparison.

The generic runtime now lets non-ego interactive actors continue past a continuation goal until their source exit, while preserving collision and off-road handling. A 30 m goal-projection ablation removed some early goal losses but put many goals far from mapped lanes, so the default remains 5 m. The r13 turning fixtures still fail due traffic-model collisions; full04 still loses an IDM actor off-road near a visible curb. Focused aerial overlays show the low-curb contacts are at real mapped boundaries, so they were not erased. Full21's source boxes overlap parked vehicles in the aerial overlay and remains excluded.

## Remaining limits before planner-gap research

- The accepted intersection is one technically faithful example, not evidence that every inD turn transfers cleanly. IDM and zero-action dynamics can still remove actors in other scenes after curb contacts or collisions; those scenes remain excluded rather than silently changed.
- The source map has priority, signs, and crosswalk semantics that the converted binary does not represent completely. The r00 scene, despite technical admission, is therefore not approved for a geographic planner-performance claim.
- The boundary audit is geometric and conservative. It identifies where boxes touch mapped curbs; the accepted scene avoids these contacts. A location-wide policy for low curbs still needs source-based validation before broad scene admission.
