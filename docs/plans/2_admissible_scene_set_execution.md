# Step 2 execution standards

These standards are fixed before changing the semantic gate or selecting research scenes.

1. **Selection:** Record source-only candidate criteria and every screened window, including the reason it fails. Require ego motion at least 15 m over the 9 s window, a reachable routed goal first entered within its 2 m success radius no earlier than source step 75, no unaudited actor crop, and independent scene keys. Interaction is measured by timed track proximity and path geometry, not actor count alone.
2. **Rules:** Inventory every regulatory element attached to an occupied ego lanelet, its sign, priority lanes, ego-footprint crosswalk contact, relevant VRUs, lane changes, and physical boundaries. For each, record source IDs and timing. A nearby sign alone is neither proof of applicability nor proof of irrelevance.
3. **Review:** Save a source-map/image overlay and a concise review report per accepted scene. Document why each rule would or would not change the safe action for the tested traffic. If evidence is ambiguous, exclude.
4. **Technical gate:** A scene must pass the current Step 1 admission in `bb_pufferdrive`; no diagnostic configuration or missing audit is accepted. Expert replay is checked until the evaluator's justified ego-goal terminal event. Ego perturbation must preserve traffic lifecycle under expert and the selected evaluator traffic controller (IDM or hybrid startup); other controllers and all-zero-control background dynamics remain recorded diagnostics, not evaluator-mode requirements.
5. **Corpus:** Reject overlapping ego windows and shared moving tracks within a recording; reserve whole recordings and preferably locations across adaptation/evaluation. Stationary parked tracks may recur in time-separated scenes, while recording remains the inference cluster. Keep a candidate-to-admission flow with counts by cause.
6. **Semantic transfer:** Verify a rule reaches the planner's actual observation, not just the JSON or binary. Otherwise admit only a scene where the source establishes that rule does not govern the tested interaction. Rule-aware model changes require US/German protocol parity.
7. **Interaction variety:** The 10-scene feasibility milestone needs at least two locations and distinct timed interactions; 50 scenes across three locations remain the completion target. A sparse turn alone does not count as evidence for yielding or VRU behavior.
8. **Research admission:** Require a current, source-linked applicability report. Test missing and stale evidence fail closed. Scores remain diagnostic until the research gate passes.

Continue independent Step 2 work after any failed candidate or audit. Request user intervention only for a genuine blocker when no independent Step 2 work remains.
