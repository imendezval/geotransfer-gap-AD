## Scope and evidence integrity

This is an offline evidence audit of the 48 frozen Workstream B/C primary rows. It preserves the saved ledger outcomes, includes the retained B fallback only where reviewer evidence is indexed, and does not change conversion, physical, map, admission, or planner gates. No source truth or physical clearance was inferred; the B/C manifests record `manual_reviews_created: false`.

The reproducible producer validated the saved converted-scene JSON, validation report, map audit, binary, physical audit, review media, raw source files, and source-map bytes. Scene/report/binary files match each case's `output_tree_sha256`; source-map bytes match both the map audit and frozen source hashes. All 48 source-context actor ID lists match converted object IDs and the scene ego index; full actor movement metadata matches the saved source recomputation. The producer verified 114 unique source files, including tracks, metadata, backgrounds, and maps, against frozen SHA-256 values. All 48 raw scene JSONs passed these structural checks: positive finite length/width/height, aligned position/velocity/heading/valid arrays, finite positions and JSON numbers, unique IDs, frozen context ID agreement, and road geometry present. Across the converted scenes there are 1,111 objects, 325 with `dimension_imputed=true`; the saved physical reports all use collision scale 1.0. These checks validate output shape and provenance, not physical truth.

## Physical findings and uncertainty

The 17 primary physical rejections contain **54 overlap-pair findings**. The audit marks 28 pairs across 16 cases `reviewable_annotation_overlap=false`; the other 26 are flagged reviewable and stationary. Two rejected rows have ego-boundary contacts, and one of those also has unreviewable pair findings. Thus 15/17 rejections are pair-only in the saved gate evidence, one has both pair and boundary markers, and one has boundary evidence without an unreviewable pair.

Of the 28 unreviewable pair findings in those 17 rows, 10 are pairs the audit marks both-stationary and 18 include a moving or unknown-motion actor. Using the disclosed 12 m ego-box-gap diagnostic, 19/28 have a gap ≤12 m and 9/28 are farther away. Four of the 16 pair-triggered rejection rows have only unreviewable pairs whose saved minimum ego-box gap is >12 m. This distance flag is descriptive; it does not clear or invalidate an overlap.

The maximum source-screen sensitivity is **15 cases**: if source review later established that every pair-only row's unreviewable overlap evidence was an annotation artifact, the physical-only rejection count could move from 17 to 2; the two saved ego-boundary-contact rows would remain flagged. This is a conditional bookkeeping bound, not a physical-truth estimate or an admission rate. Pair truth remains unreviewed, and geometry, task/rule, and traffic results remain as recorded.

The six primary review-blocked rows plus the retained B fallback have 15/15 overlap pairs flagged `reviewable_annotation_overlap=true` and both-stationary. No pair has been cleared and no human review was created. The three geometry rejects have six concurrent overlap findings (four reviewable stationary pairs and two unreviewable pairs); one also has ego-boundary contacts. Their route result remains independently failed.

Across the 48 primaries plus the fallback, the audits contain 84 pair-in-case overlap occurrences and 51 unique `(recording, actor pair)` identities. Nineteen identities recur within the same recording, spanning 52 occurrences; all 19 are marked both-stationary in every recurrence. This is repeated saved source-box evidence, not 19 independent conflicts or a population estimate.

Endpoint flags across those 84 pair occurrences (168 endpoints; categories overlap) are: ego 1, designated task actor 2, near the ego by the saved ≤12 m timed center-distance catalog 38, farther/not in that catalog 129, moving 20, stationary 148. At the pair level, 64/84 are both-stationary and 20 include a moving or unknown-motion actor. These labels help route review; they do not decide eligibility.
